When I left my $400K job at Google in 2021, my manager told me I would be back in six months.

I had 14 months of runway, a Notion doc with 31 startup ideas, and a wife who had agreed to exactly one of them.

The one she agreed to made $0 for 9 months. The one she vetoed is now a company with 40 employees, run by someone I met at a conference in Austin who had the same idea and nobody to veto it.

My manager was wrong about the six months. It was 26. I am back at Google, two levels higher, and I still have the Notion doc.

The 31st idea was "a Google for startup ideas." Nobody has built it.
